FAQ

Common Questions

1

How do I book a session?

Simply browse our tutor directory, find a match, and click 'Book Session' on their profile. You can schedule based on their available slots.

2

What if I need a refund?

We offer a 100% money-back guarantee for your first session if you're not satisfied. Contact support within 24 hours of the session.

3

How do I become a verified tutor?

Submit an application via the 'Tutor Application' subject in the contact form. We require proof of qualifications and a brief interview process.
